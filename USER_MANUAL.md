# HotelOS – Role-Based Enterprise User Manual

This manual is integrated directly into the HotelOS Web Application UI at `/manual` and provides end-to-end guidance for all 15 operational roles.

Please refer to the full manual documentation in:
- In-UI Application: `http://localhost:3000/manual`
- Master Reference: `HOTELOS_ROLE_BASED_USER_MANUAL.md`

### Key Roles Documented:
1. IT Administrator & SuperAdmin (Masters, Forms, Workflows, Numbering Sequences, DB Maintenance)
2. Front Office & Reception (Room Grid, Check-in, Checkout, Folios, Requests)
3. Reservations & Revenue Management (Booking engine, availability, rate plans)
4. Guest Relations & VIP CRM (Guest directory, preferences, loyalty)
5. Housekeeping & Laundry Management (Status board, attendant assignment, linen par levels)
6. Restaurant POS & F&B (Floor tables, live POS billing, room charge)
7. Kitchen Display System - KDS (Queue, prep timers, bump order)
8. Banquets & Event Sales (Venues, event packages, function sheets)
9. Purchase & Vendor Procurement (Requisitions, purchase orders, vendor directory)
10. Inventory & Stores (Item master, stock transactions, stores ledger)
11. Finance & Night Audit (General ledger, journal vouchers, invoices, EOD audit)
12. HR & Staff Management (Staff directory, shift attendance)
13. Engineering & Maintenance (Work orders, asset registry)
14. Security & Loss Prevention (Visitor log, gate passes, incident reports)
15. Reports & Analytics Hub (Manager flash, RevPAR, ADR, turnover metrics)
